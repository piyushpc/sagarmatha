Anti-termite treatment

Termites — the "silent destroyer" — hollow out wood and structures from the inside. Our anti-termite treatment creates a protective chemical barrier that keeps them out. We offer two types:

Pre-Construction Anti-Termite Treatment

Applied before and during construction to protect the building from subterranean termite attacks from day one — for the entire life of the property.

Treatment stages
  • Site inspection & soil assessment
  • Soil preparation before foundation
  • Foundation trench treatment
  • Column footing treatment
  • Plinth filling treatment
  • Backfill soil treatment
  • Wall–floor junction treatment
  • Pipe entry point treatment
  • Expansion joint treatment
  • External perimeter soil treatment
  • Final barrier inspection

Post-Construction Anti-Termite Treatment

Used when a building is already constructed and shows termite activity. Chemicals are injected through precisely drilled points to stop the infestation and protect the structure.

Areas covered
  • Floor
  • Door frames
  • Wooden flooring
  • Walls
  • Window frames
  • Garden area
  • Kitchen
  • Wooden furniture
  • Boundary wall
  • Bathroom
  • False ceiling
