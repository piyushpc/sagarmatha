Anti-termite treatment

Termites are called the "silent destroyer" because they eat away wood and buildings from the inside, often without you noticing. Our anti-termite treatment builds a protective barrier to keep them out. We offer two types:

Pre-Construction Anti-Termite Treatment

Done before and during construction, so your building is protected from termites from day one — for as long as it stands.

Treatment stages
  • Site inspection & soil assessment
  • Soil preparation before foundation
  • Foundation trench treatment
  • Column footing treatment
  • Plinth filling treatment
  • Backfill soil treatment
  • Wall–floor junction treatment
  • Pipe entry point treatment
  • Expansion joint treatment
  • External perimeter soil treatment
  • Final barrier inspection

Post-Construction Anti-Termite Treatment

For buildings that already have termites. We drill small holes and inject treatment to stop the termites and protect the structure.

Areas covered
  • Floor
  • Door frames
  • Wooden flooring
  • Walls
  • Window frames
  • Garden area
  • Kitchen
  • Wooden furniture
  • Boundary wall
  • Bathroom
  • False ceiling
